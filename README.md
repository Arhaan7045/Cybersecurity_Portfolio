- **[Projects](Projects/)** — substantial, reproducible projects as they are completed.

## How I document practical work

Each episode should distinguish between:
- **What I did:** commands, tools, and steps actually performed.
- **What I observed:** real output or behavior, with sensitive data redacted.
- **What I concluded:** interpretation supported by the evidence.
- **Limitations:** what the evidence does not establish.
- **Verification:** a mini-challenge or repeatable check.

I do not invent command output or claim mastery based only on reading. Work is performed only in systems and labs I own or am authorized to test.

## Evidence and privacy

Before publishing, remove passwords, tokens, private IPs where sensitive, personal information, account identifiers, and any data from systems you do not have permission to disclose. Explain what each screenshot demonstrates and what it cannot prove.
